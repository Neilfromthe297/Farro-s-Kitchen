Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what personal details La Granja collects when you order on this website, why we need them, and what we do with them. We only ask for what we need to prepare your order.

Who we are

La Granja, Zandstraat 2, Oranjestad, Aruba. Phone: +297 582 2065. We are responsible for the personal details you share on this website.

What we collect

When you place an order, we receive:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • your order: dishes, sides, sizes and total;
  • how you will pick up (inside or drive-thru), your pickup time and, if you choose to tell us, your car description;
  • any special instructions you write, such as allergies;
  • the language you used on the website.

We do not collect card or bank details on this website. You pay when you pick up your order.

Why we use it

  • to prepare your order and have it ready at the time you chose;
  • to contact you about your order, for example to confirm a large order or if something is unavailable;
  • to take allergies and other instructions into account;
  • to help if you have a question or complaint about an order.

We do not sell your details, and we do not use them for advertising or send you marketing messages.

Who else handles your details

  • Web3Forms delivers your order from the website to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider stores the order emails we receive.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website. Like most websites, its servers may briefly record technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep it secure.
  • Google Maps shows the map of our location. When the map loads, Google may receive your IP address and similar technical data, under Google's own privacy policy.

These services may store data outside Aruba.

Cookies and storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics. Your phone or computer only remembers the language you chose (English, Spanish or Papiamento) and, for 12 hours after you order, a copy of your last order so you can show the receipt at pickup. This stays on your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep order emails only as long as we need them to handle your order and any questions about it, and no longer than 12 months. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the details we have about you, to correct them, or to delete them. Call us at +297 582 2065 or ask at the restaurant.

Security

This website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection, and only La Granja staff who handle orders can read the order emails.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 16. Please have a parent or guardian place the order.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
