About

The story behind our dishes

What makes us stand out

At the heart of La Granja is a fusion of authentic Peruvian heritage and the warmth of a family-friendly dining experience. Our dedication to bringing genuine Latin American flavors is evident in every dish we serve. We pride ourselves on not just cooking food but on crafting an authentic culinary experience that celebrates our roots.

What truly distinguishes us is our commitment to creating a welcoming atmosphere that feels like home. La Granja is more than a dining spot; it’s a place where families and friends come together, fostering connections in every meal. Our journey began right here in Aruba, and we remain proudly rooted in Oranjestad. We continually evolve to meet our customers’ diverse tastes while staying true to our Latin American roots.

Our mission statement

Our mission is to quickly serve our customers homestyle food. We are dedicated to preparing and serving high-quality, flavorful Latin American flavors in a welcoming environment, where every meal is an opportunity to celebrate culture. Our commitment extends beyond the plate, as we strive to be a positive, contributing member of every community we serve.
